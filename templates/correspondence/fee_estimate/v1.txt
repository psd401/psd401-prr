This charge is calculated from the District's adopted fee schedule under
RCW 42.56.120. It covers the cost of copying the records only. The District
does not charge you for the time its staff spend locating, reviewing, or
preparing records, and it is not a charge for inspecting them.
{% if deposit_amount %}
Before the District does that copying work it asks for a deposit of
{{ deposit_amount }}. RCW 42.56.120(2) allows a deposit of up to ten
percent of the estimated charge. The deposit is applied to the charge; it is
not an additional cost. The District will hold work on this request until the
deposit is received.
{% endif %}
To pay, contact the Public Records Officer at {{ pra_officer_email }} and the
District will confirm the payment methods it accepts and where to send
payment. Tell us in the same message if you would like to narrow the request
to reduce the charge, or to inspect the records instead.

If the District does not hear from you, it may stop work on this request and
may close it under RCW 42.56.120(2). The District will write to you before it
does so.
