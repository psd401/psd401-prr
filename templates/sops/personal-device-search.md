# SOP TEMPLATE: Personal Device / Personal Account Search Directive
**Status: DRAFT — counsel review required before first use.**

## Why this exists
Under *Nissen v. Pierce County*, 183 Wn.2d 863 (2015), records about district
business on an employee's PERSONAL phone or account are public records. The
district cannot search the device itself; the employee searches in good faith
and attests. This SOP is that procedure.

## When triage fires this
Requests matching the `personal-devices` registry entry, or any request where
the subject matter plausibly lived in texts (athletics, board, investigations).

## Procedure
1. **Directive.** PRA officer sends the search directive (template below) to
   each identified employee, with a response deadline (suggested: 5 business
   days) logged as a CollectionTask.
2. **Employee search.** Employee searches texts/messaging apps/personal email
   for the terms and date range provided.
3. **Production.** Responsive items exported as screenshots or message exports
   and submitted to the request file. Purely personal content on the same
   thread may be redacted by the employee, but the record itself comes in.
4. **Affidavit.** Employee signs the good-faith search declaration (below) —
   including when NOTHING responsive was found. The affidavits ARE the
   adequate-search documentation.
5. **Non-response.** Escalate to supervisor at deadline; document everything.

## Directive email template (counsel to finalize)
> Subject: Public Records Search Directive — [PRR number]
> The district has received a public records request that may include text
> messages or personal-account communications about district business.
> Search terms: [terms]. Date range: [range]. Under Nissen v. Pierce County,
> such records are public records regardless of device ownership. Please
> search, submit any responsive items, and return the attached declaration
> by [date]. Contact [PRA officer] with questions.

## Declaration template (counsel to finalize)
> I searched my personal devices and accounts for records responsive to
> [PRR number] using the terms and date range provided. I have produced all
> responsive records / found no responsive records (circle one). I understand
> records about district business are public records under RCW 42.56.
> Signed / dated.

## Prevention (policy recommendations)
- District-business-on-district-systems policy for staff AND board members.
- Board members use district email accounts exclusively.
- Coaches use district-visible channels (ParentSquare) instead of personal
  texting/team apps where feasible.
